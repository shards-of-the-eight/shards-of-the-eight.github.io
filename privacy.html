Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. This policy covers the web game Shards of the Eight (shards-of-the-eight.github.io).

The short version

No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your game progress stays on your device. We never see your name, email, contacts, location or card details.

What stays on your device

Your heroes, items, settings and progress are saved only on your device (in your browser's storage). Deleting the game or clearing its data deletes them. We cannot see or recover them.

Purchases

If you buy something in the Shop, the payment is handled by our payment provider on its own page. It processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never receive your card details.

To give you what you bought, our payment server keeps: a random purchase code created by your device, the items it owns, and the payment provider's reference for each purchase. This code is not linked to your name. Our server provider (Cloudflare) processes your IP address to deliver requests and to stop abuse; we do not store it.

The purchase code lets you restore purchases on another device (Shop > Restore). Keep it private.

Arena duels and moving heroes

When you duel a friend or move heroes between devices, the two devices connect through our relay server on Cloudflare using a random 6-letter code. It passes messages between the devices while they are connected and stores nothing. In a duel, your opponent sees your hero's name, looks, stats and deck. When moving heroes, your save passes through the relay to your other device. A photo you take to scan a player card is read on your phone and is never sent anywhere. A player card's QR code holds your hero's name and a one-time code.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone. Players under 18 should ask a parent or guardian before buying anything.

Your choices

You can delete all game data at any time (Settings > Reset all, or delete the game). To have the purchase record for your purchase code deleted, contact us with the code.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes. Questions: shardsoftheeight.game@outlook.com
