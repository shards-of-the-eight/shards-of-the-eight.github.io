The game

  • Pick one of 8 classes (Warrior, Mage, Archer, Fay, Wielder, Vampire, Werewolf, Shielder) and create your hero.
  • Build a deck from card packs and fight Magic-style card battles with mana, spells and creatures.
  • Walk deeper into the wilds for better loot, or follow the story to win back the Eight Shards.
  • Merge cards and gear to make them stronger, and master the eight elements.
  • Duel a friend in the Arena with a 6-letter code.
